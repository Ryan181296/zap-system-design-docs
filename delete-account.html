ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION REQUEST

Instructions on how to request the permanent deletion of your ZAP CRM account and associated personal data, in accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), Google Play User Data Policy, and Vietnam Decree 13/2023/ND-CP.

Data Privacy & Deletion Apple & Google Compliant Effective: 2026

1. How to Delete Your Account In-App (Recommended)

You can initiate account deletion directly inside the ZAP mobile application at any time:
1
Open ZAP CRM App & Log In
Launch the app and make sure you are logged into the account you wish to delete.
2
Navigate to Profile / Account Settings
Tap on the Profile (Cá nhân) or Settings (Cài đặt) tab located at the bottom navigation bar.
3
Select "Account Security" → "Delete Account"
Tap on Delete Account (Xóa tài khoản) at the bottom of the security settings screen.
4
Confirm Deletion
Read the consequences of deletion and confirm with your authentication method (OTP / Password).

2. Request Account Deletion via Email / Support Form

If you no longer have access to the mobile application or wish to submit a manual deletion request, you can contact our Data Protection Officer:
📩 Submit a Request via Email
Send an email from your registered email address or include your registered phone number to:
📧 Send Deletion Request (support@zap.vn)
Please specify: (1) Your registered phone number, (2) Full Name, and (3) Reason for deletion (optional).

3. What Data Is Deleted & Retention Timeline

🗑️ Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • Personal Profile: Full name, phone number, email address, avatar, OAuth links (Google/Facebook).
  • Authentication Tokens: Active sessions, push notification tokens, device identifiers.
  • CRM & Business Data: Customer lists, notes, and activity history linked strictly to your personal user account.

⏳ Processing Timeline & Legal Retention

  • Processing Period: Account deletion requests are processed and verified within 30 days.
  • Legal & Tax Exceptions: In accordance with Vietnamese accounting and tax regulations, transaction invoices, billing history, and completed payment records may be retained in encrypted archive logs for the statutory period required by law (usually 5 to 10 years for financial audit compliance).
